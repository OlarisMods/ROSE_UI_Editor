====================================================================
  ROSE UI EDITOR -- your ROSE Online interface, in your colours
====================================================================

  Double-click "ROSE UI Editor.pyw".  Three steps: find the game, pick two
  colours, apply.

  (For now this needs Python 3 from python.org -- tick "tcl/tk" if the
  installer asks, it usually does it for you. A one-file version for
  everyone is planned.)


  UPDATED FOR THE 16 OCTOBER 2026 PATCH. Themes are built on the
  patched interface, and now also colour the equipment slots, the
  chat tabs and the new party buttons. Applying a theme also clears
  out a file older UI packs left behind, which would otherwise hide
  the patch's new icons -- Undo puts it back if you ever want it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE THREE STEPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1  YOUR GAME     It looks in the usual places. If it doesn't find ROSE,
                 use Browse and pick the folder with trose.exe in it.

2  YOUR COLOURS  Main   -- panels, glass and frames
                 Accent -- edges, highlights and text
                 Click a colour to open the picker; the preview follows as
                 you drag, and Cancel puts the old colour back.

                 Or press "Surprise me" for a matched pair. Every surprise
                 has a SEED -- a word like "ember" or "tidepool". Type any
                 word, name or number as a seed and press Use: the same seed
                 always gives the same theme, so "pumpkin" is always
                 pumpkin. Share the word, and anyone gets your colours.

                 The released themes are seeds too: type void, crimson,
                 moldie or ancient for their colours.

                 Health and mana bars:
                   Classic         red and blue, as always
                   Match my theme  health takes your main, mana your accent

                 Chat colours:
                   Classic         the game's own channel colours
                   Match my theme  every channel in colours that suit your
                                   theme -- always readable, always different
                                   from each other

3  APPLY         Close ROSE first. Then Apply, and start the game.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT NEVER CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Some colours MEAN something, and no theme touches them:
    staff names, error messages, quest types, the low-health flash, the
    good / bad / caution colours in tooltips, and every class's own buff
    colour. Chat channels keep the game's own colours unless you choose
    Match -- and even then they are kept well clear of every staff colour,
    so no player's name can look like a GM's.

If you pick two colours so close that health and mana would look alike,
the app says so and won't apply until you change one.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
UNDO AND REMOVE -- NOTHING IS EVER LOST
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Before it changes anything, the app copies every file it is about to
replace into its own folder, and writes down every file it adds:

    C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\ROSE Theme\

    (Still named "ROSE Theme", the app's old name, so backups made by
    earlier versions keep working with Undo and Remove.)

    Undo last change   back to how things were before your last Apply
    Remove theme       back to how things were before this app EVER
                       touched the game -- including any other mods
                       you had installed

If the app is ever interrupted mid-apply -- a crash, a power cut -- it
puts the game folder back the next time you open it.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------
"ROSE is running"         close the game, then try again
"won't let this app write" run the app as administrator
Anything else              the message says what happened; nothing is
                           changed unless Apply finishes
